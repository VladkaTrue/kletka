Questions

Is everything really included?

Yes. There is nothing to buy in the app. The tip jar on this site is optional and changes nothing in the game.

Does it work offline?

Fully. Grids are built into the app; Game Center catches up when you are back online.

Will I lose my streak if I change phones?

No. History and streak sync through your own iCloud and come back after a reinstall.

How are levels decided?

By the hardest technique a grid needs. Easy needs only singles; Expert needs chains of harder steps such as X‑wing and swordfish.

Is the daily grid the same for everyone?

Yes. Four a day, one per level, and they change at midnight UTC.

Which devices and languages?

iPhone and iPad with iOS 17 or later. English and Russian.
